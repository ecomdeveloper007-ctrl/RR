How to read a campaign result

Advertising dashboards can feel technical. This short guide explains the terms used throughout the portfolio, so the numbers are easy to follow at a glance.

Impressions

The total number of times an ad was shown. One person can see it more than once.

Reach

The estimated number of individual people who saw the ad at least once.

Lead

A person who showed interest by completing a form or sharing contact details.

Landing page view

A visitor who clicked and waited for the page to load — a stronger signal than a click alone.

Cost per result

The average spend needed to achieve the main campaign action, such as one lead.

CPC

Cost per click. The average amount spent when someone clicks an ad.

CPL

Cost per lead. The average amount spent to generate one lead.

Amount spent

The total money invested in the campaign during the reporting period shown.

Budget

The planned daily or total spend allowed. Actual spend can come in lower.

Viewable impression

An impression that met the platform's standard for being visible to a viewer.

YouTube public view

A video view reported publicly on YouTube, showing how much attention it earned.

Attribution window

The period after a view or click in which a result can still be credited to the ad.
